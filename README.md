Mindustry, but you play it like Vampire Survivors.

You get dropped on a random map with no core and nothing to build. Enemies come at you from every side and you have to survive 50 waves.

## Install

You need Mindustry v8 on PC. It doesn't work on phones yet.

- **Easiest:** in the game, open **Mods**, press **Mod Browser**, search for Mindustry Survivors and install it.
- **If it's not there:** in **Mods**, press **Import From GitHub** and type `kennyzhang0819/mindustry-survivors`.
- **By hand:** download `MindustrySurvivors.jar` from [Releases](https://github.com/kennyzhang0819/mindustry-survivors/releases), then in **Mods** press **Open Folder** and drop it in.

Restart the game after installing.

## How to play

Press **Mindustry Survivors** on the main menu, pick a difficulty and hit **New Game**.

- Move with WASD, aim with the mouse and hold left click to shoot.
- Kill enemies and they drop gems. Walk over them to level up.
- Every level up you pick 1 of 3 cards: a new turret, a new passive, or a level for one you already have.
- Turrets fly around you and shoot wherever you shoot. You can have 6 turrets and 6 passives.
- Passives are named after buildings. Some help you run and shoot from far away, others let you tank hits up close.
- Sometimes an enemy drops glowing Thorium that heals you, or a magnet that pulls every gem on the map to you.
- Enemies hit harder every wave. If you die, the run's over.
- Beat all 50 waves to win. After that you can keep going in Endless, where it just keeps getting harder.
- Quitting saves your run. Press **Continue run** to pick it back up.

## Difficulty

Casual, Easy, Normal, Hard and Eradication change how many enemies show up. On easier ones each gem is worth more, so you still level up about as fast.

Want more pain? Eradication +1, +2 and +3 add curses, which are random nasty rules for the enemies. They show under your level bar, hover one to see what it does.

## Settings

In **Settings** you can pick which turrets you get and which enemies come: Serpulo, Erekir or both. It kicks in on your next run.

There's also an Admin tab if you want to cheat or just mess around.

## Good to know

- It doesn't touch your campaign or your other saves.
- Single player only.
- Balance is still a work in progress, so expect some turrets to be way better than others.
